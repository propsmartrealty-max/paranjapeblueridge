## FEMA Regulations and Repatriation of Funds

Investing in India is highly streamlined for NRIs under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA):
- **Eligibility**: NRIs, Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs), and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) can freely purchase residential properties in India without requiring special permissions from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Funding**: Payments must be made via inward remittance through regular banking channels or through NRE/NRO/FCNR accounts.
- **Repatriation**: NRIs can easily repatriate the sale proceeds of up to two residential properties, provided the funds originally came from an NRE or FCNR account. Rental income can be fully repatriated after applicable tax deductions via an NRO account.

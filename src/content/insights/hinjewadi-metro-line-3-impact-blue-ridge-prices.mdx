# Hinjewadi Metro Line 3 — Impact on Blue Ridge Property Prices in 2026 & Beyond

The Pune Metro Line 3 — connecting **Hinjewadi Phase 1 to Shivajinagar** via Balewadi, Aundh, and Baner — is the most consequential infrastructure development for West Pune real estate in a generation. For residents and investors at Paranjape Blue Ridge, its implications are profound and multi-dimensional.

---

## What is Pune Metro Line 3?

Pune Metro Line 3 (also called the Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar Metro Corridor) is a **23.3 km elevated metro rail line** being developed under PPP mode by Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (PMRDA). It has **23 stations** running from Hinjewadi Phase 3 through Phase 2, Phase 1, Balewadi, Baner, Aundh, and into Shivajinagar.

### Key Facts (2026 Status)
- **Total Length:** 23.3 km
- **Stations:** 23 (fully elevated)
- **Expected Completion:** Hinjewadi Phase 1–Balewadi by late 2027; Full corridor by 2029
- **Technology:** Standard gauge, third rail electrification
- **Daily Ridership (Projected):** 4–6 lakh passengers by 2030

## Why the Metro is a Game-Changer for Blue Ridge

### 1. Elimination of the Hinjewadi Bottleneck

Hinjewadi's main pain point has historically been traffic congestion — especially on the Hinjewadi–Wakad bridge and the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park access roads during peak hours. The Metro Line 3 introduces **rapid, congestion-free transit** that eliminates this friction for:

- IT employees commuting to Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, and other park tenants
- Residents accessing Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, and central Pune without a car
- Children travelling to schools outside the township
- Domestic helps, staff, and service providers accessing the township

### 2. Premium Connectivity to Central Pune

Once the full corridor is operational, Blue Ridge residents will have:
- **12 minutes** to Balewadi (Balewadi High Street)
- **18 minutes** to Aundh
- **28 minutes** to Shivajinagar (City Centre)

This transforms Blue Ridge from a "Hinjewadi-specific" address to a **pan-Pune lifestyle destination** accessible from anywhere along the corridor.

### 3. Expanded Tenant Base = Higher Rental Yields

Currently, Blue Ridge draws corporate tenants primarily from IT companies in Hinjewadi Phases 1–3. With metro connectivity, the township will attract professionals working anywhere along the metro corridor — including Balewadi, Baner, and Aundh. This expands the **rental demand catchment area** dramatically.

**Projected rental yield improvement:** from current 4.5–5.2% to **5.5–6.5%** post-metro operationalisation.

The enactment of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act in 2016, and the subsequent establishment of the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA), fundamentally transformed the property market in Pune. It shifted the industry from a developer-dominated landscape to a highly transparent, consumer-protected fortress.

Today, Maharashtra is widely recognized as having the most robust and proactive RERA implementation in the country. For homebuyers and investors—especially NRIs investing from abroad—MahaRERA acts as an uncompromising financial and legal shield.

In this guide, we break down the critical mechanisms of MahaRERA and how they guarantee the safety of your capital.

## 1. The Escrow Account Mandate (Financial Security)

Historically, the biggest risk in real estate was the diversion of funds. Developers would often collect capital for Project A and divert it to acquire land for Project B, leading to massive construction delays and stalled projects.

MahaRERA completely eradicated this practice through the **Escrow Account Mandate**.
- By law, a developer must deposit exactly **70% of all funds collected from homebuyers** (for a specific project) into a separate, dedicated escrow bank account.
- These funds can only be withdrawn to cover the construction and land cost of *that specific project*.
- Furthermore, withdrawals must be certified by an independent engineer, an architect, and a chartered accountant, proportional to the percentage of completion.

This ironclad financial discipline guarantees that the project has the liquidity required to reach completion.

## 2. Standardization of Carpet Area (No More "Super Built-Up")

Before RERA, developers would sell properties based on ambiguous metrics like "Super Built-Up Area" or "Salable Area," which included the proportionate area of common amenities, lobbies, and staircases. Buyers were often shocked to find the actual livable space was 30% smaller than they anticipated.

MahaRERA standardized the definition of **Carpet Area**: The net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding external walls, service shafts, and exclusive balconies. 
Under the law, a developer can only quote prices and sell units based strictly on this defined RERA Carpet Area. You pay exactly for the space you can walk on inside your home.

## 3. Strict Timelines and Delayed Possession Penalties

Delayed possession was the bane of the pre-RERA real estate sector. Today, when a project is registered, the developer must declare a strict possession timeline.

If the developer fails to hand over the property by the stipulated date, MahaRERA grants the homebuyer two powerful rights:
1. **Withdrawal:** The buyer can withdraw from the project, and the developer is legally bound to refund the entire amount paid, plus interest.
2. **Compensation:** If the buyer chooses to stay in the project, the developer must pay interest for every month of delay until the handover occurs.

## 4. Five-Year Defect Liability Period

The protection doesn't end when you receive the keys. Under Section 14 of the RERA Act, the developer is legally obligated to rectify any structural defects or poor workmanship discovered within **5 years** from the date of handing over possession. These rectifications must be done free of charge, within 30 days of the complaint being filed.

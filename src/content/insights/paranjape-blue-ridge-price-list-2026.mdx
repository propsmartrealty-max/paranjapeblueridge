## Price Factors to Note

### Floor Rise
Floor rise typically adds **₹50–150 per sq. ft.** per floor above podium level. Higher floors command a premium for views and privacy.

### Preferential Location Charges (PLC)
- **River-facing PLC:** Additional 3–5% on base price
- **Golf-facing PLC:** Additional 2–4% on base price
- **Corner Unit PLC:** Additional 1–3% on base price

### Applicable Taxes & Charges
- GST: 5% on base price (for under-construction units)
- Stamp Duty: 6% (Maharashtra)
- Registration: 1% of agreement value
- Club Membership, Maintenance Deposit: As per builder norms
